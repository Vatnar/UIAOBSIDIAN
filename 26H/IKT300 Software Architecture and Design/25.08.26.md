## Conceptual integrity
Quality of the system where all the concepts and realtionships with each other are applied in a consistent way throughout the system. For instance in regards in to accept and cancel buttons. Sticking to common solutions. 

Small team for architecture, not many people. Which would lead to low conceptual integrity. 
### Tradeoff between maintainability vs time to market.

### Architectual vs detailed design
Functional requirements is orthogonal to non functional requirements. 
Specification (what) vs design (how)
Domain/application specificy

## Design documentation
- IEEE 1016 has a general good structure

### Design rationale.
Often not documented
- Captures the reasoning of the designer that led to the system as designed and the justifications of those decisions.

Might be important, for instance if the world changed, and your prior good decisions is now viewed as bad decisions. 
May include concerns, trade-offs, design options, decisions made, and arguments and justifications made to reach a decision.
