### Design rationale.
Often not documented
- Captures the reasoning of the designer that led to the system as designed and the justifications of those decisions.

Might be important, for instance if the world changed, and your prior good decisions is now viewed as bad decisions. 
May include concerns, trade-offs, design options, decisions made, and arguments and justifications made to reach a decision.

### Coupling and Cohesion
#### Coupling 
- an indication of how much two components dependent on each other to function properly. 
Coupling is inevitable, but we need to make sure we know what is coupled, and want to reduce it. 

#### Cohesion
- an indiciation of how well to modules fit together. Usually achieved by assigning a single functionality to components. 
Putting related objects together. And avoiding putting unrelated stuff together.

We want Low coupling and high cohesion.

### Abstraction and refine
- Abstraction - a description of something that omits details that are not relevant to the purpose of the abstract -> to generalize.

- Refinement - a detailed description that conforms to another (its abstraction).
