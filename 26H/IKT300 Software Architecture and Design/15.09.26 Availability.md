"It works on my machine"
- Property of software that is there and ready to carry out its task when you need it to be. 
- System to mark or repair faults such that they do not become failures.
- Builds on reliability by adding recovery. Minimize service outage time by mitigating faults.

Faults can be
- prevented,
- tolerated,
- removed,
- forecast.

We are concerned with
- How faults are detected,
- how frequent are they,
- what happens when they occur,
- how long a system may be out of operation,
- how faults or failures can be prevented,
- what notifications are require when a failure occurs.

Measure:
MTBF: Mean time between failures
MTTR: Mean time to repair
Availability caluclated as (MTBF)/(MTBF + MTTR)
Source: Where does it come from
Stimulus: Something is missing
Artifact: What failed
Environment: How it may behave when its already in a failure state
