Requirements:
- Functional requirements
	- What the system most do. How it behaves or react.
- Quality attribute requirements
	- Annotate functional requirements. (Non-functional). How fast a function should be performed, how resilient to erroneous input, how easy to learn, etc.
- Constraints
	- Zero degrees of freedom decisions. Design decision that has already been made.

Functionality is the ability of the system to do the work for which it was intended.
Functionality has a strange relationship to architecture:
- Functionality does not determine architecture. 
QA: 
Performance, availability, usability.
